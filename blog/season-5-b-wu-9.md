10 - Matthew (NEW) - League A - Last 5: 47 (W), 42 (W), 23 (L), 40 (L), 50 (W) - Okay instead of just listing who returned for me this week or whatever maybe I'll try to talk about the trends and such and how I came to these decisions instead of just waving my hands and going **points!** Okay anyway, this last slot was pretty tough to choose but I decided to prioritize a few things when choosing between similar teams: consistency, pop off potential, and trend line. So, 6/8 of my scores have been 40+ which means I pretty much fulfill the consistency criteria, especially considering my recent 23 pointer was so heavily influenced by the fact my entire team just died during the international break. And I also fulfill the trend line criteria, considering my points have increased across the past few weeks which (maybe) means that my players (/the teams my players play for) are in form. Regardless, a win in the cup and the league for me this week and a 50 pointer for the first time in a bit.

9 - Gavin (-6) - League B - Last 5: 38 (W), 52 (W), 28 (L), 29 (L), 48 (W) - Gavin's the first of an interesting group of managers who have a pretty solid score 4 weeks ago, 2 bad weeks, and then a solid score this week. Which doesn't really fulfill that consistency criteria, but Gavin's edge comes from the fact that he _was_ consistent. 4 of his first 5 weeks were 50+ (which is just kinda insane, those are Matthew2 numbers) and he could _technically_ blame last week's loss on benching Isak (which he is fortunate didn't cost him again this week), but what really matters is the score in my `Weekly Scores` tab of the spreadsheet, so... Anyway, I think we need a few more weeks of data to see exactly where Gavin sits and if he gets back to his previous heights, but for now, a solid start and a quarterfinal appearance for him as well.

8 - Chris (+2) - League A - Last 5: 39 (L), 43 (W), 46 (W), 46 (W), 37 (W) - And unlike Gavin's past few, Chris _does_ have the consistency (but doesn't quite have the upward trend line this past week). Still, he's been averaging ~42 over the last 5 weeks which definitely gives him a chance any given week. And not only has it given him a chance, he's got 4 wins in a row heading into a match against a very in-form Jesse(who didn't make this list (yet), sorry. beat Chris and you'll be here though). The only downside of Chris's consistency is that his highest score and only 50+ pointer came in week 1, and it cost him with a loss in the cup this week to Amber's 52 pointer. I think Chris is in a similar position to Gavin, where it'll be interesting to see if he can pick up some less-clenching wins in the coming weeks.

7 - ChrisB (NEW) - League B - Last 5: 44 (L), 68 (W), 35 (W), 32 (W), 46 (W) - Don't look too closely at ChrisB's scores vs Gavin's, they are _very_ similar. Although, Chris's are just a little bit better. He has a 68 pointer just 4 weeks ago, a second week in the 40s, and _won_ both of his off-weeks. Well, in the league. The cup was a different story, where his 32 point week cost him his spot in the Round of 16 as he fell to Kim's 42 pointer. Meanwhile, Gavin had a bye and dodged what would have been putting up the 2nd lowest score that round. Either way, Chris's league form has seen him rocket up the table, now level on points with Gavin at the top, so obviously he's doing something right. A (decently) high floor, a very high ceiling, and ticking upward heading into a huge match against Amber this week.

6 - Amber (NEW) - League B - Last 5: 71 (W), 25 (L), 33 (W), 60 (W), 52 (W) - Speaking of... Amber joins the list after putting up back to back League B high scores (and being the first manager in _any_ league to do so). She's just about the definition of a boom or bust team, as the only manager to have 3 60+ scores this season, she also has 2 scores in the 20s and _0_ in the 40s. Which is definitely interesting. And definitely makes her a scary team to go up against any given week, which is kinda what Power Rankings are all about. Anyway, definitely a team with pop off potential and on an upward trend, which makes me glad to be on the opposite side of the cup bracket from her.
