5 - ChrisB (+2) - League B - Last 5: 68 (W), 35 (W), 32 (W), 46 (W), 51 (W) - That's 5 wins in a row for ChrisB as he claims the "longest winstreak in the leagues" trophy this week after other Chris's streak was broken. And with the 2nd highest score in League B as well (by 1 point), it was enough to keep him trending upward on this list. Which he should be happy to see compared to his competitor at the top of League B who has taken up residency in the "fell off" section.

I guess I'm in 3rd on that table though so you can take that fact how you want. _I_ think it's probably the most important table there is.

3 - Andrew (-2) - League B - Last 5: 41 (W), 50 (W), 59 (W), 43 (L), 45 (L) - Remember how I mentioned that luck is a skill? Well, Andrew might need to get to practicing. (MatthewR and Zach both have 2 in League A, while Maddie has a draw and a loss at 40+ in League C, if that counts) Either way, Andrew's only spent 2 weeks out of 9 this season below 30, so I think he'll be fine, but also back to back losses while scoring in the 40s is wild.

2 - Dylan (+1) - League A - 38 (L), 51 (W), 41 (W), 54 (L), 53 (W) - And onto the other manager who has seen some bad luck in the past couple of weeks, Dylan! Anyway, he's entered the echelon of impressive runs that without a doubt deserve to be near the top of the list, so it's only fighting for the top spot from here.
