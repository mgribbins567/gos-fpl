# The League of Champions

And... welcome to the League of Champions! Unfortunately, we couldn't secure the rights to use the _other_ tournament's name, so this will have to do. Plus, it kinda fits the vibe of using an unofficial website to do this whole thing. Anyway, I haven't decided on the full scope of the tournament as far as the knockout stage goes, but I do know that we will start with:

### The League of Champions Group Stage

A 36-team table. 6 matches. 12 teams eliminated. _0_ matches against managers in your league.

I mean, that about sums it up. And if you want something more verbose: over the next 6 gameweeks, each of you will play against 3 managers from each other league. After those 6 weeks, the bottom 12 teams will be eliminated, 9th-24th will go into a single gameweek playoff, while 1st-8th will secure their place in the Round of 16.

And this week has some great matchups, headlined by a Season 4 League B rivalry match between John and Mack! Elsewhere, Darryan takes on League B's Haaland owner Kim, Jesse and Morgan roll back the years and go up against one another after a bit of a hiatus, and we'll see a couple of League B debutant vs League C debutant matchups in Cole vs Charlotte and Angus vs Maddie!
