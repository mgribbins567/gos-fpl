# Power Rankings

On the bubble (in no order): Scott, Coop, MatthewR, Delaney, Anthony
Fell off: Anthony

10 - Tori (NEW) - League C - Last 2: 46 (W), 52 (W) - A pair of wins and solid scores from our League C #1 see her take a spot on this list with quite the point spread this week. Only one double digit coming from Calafiori and a whole lotta 4s and 5s. Hill and Truffert mirrored one another with 4 pointers, Rice and Rogers both had 5, and Roefs and Ballard picked up their assists (and Ballard got his weekly 10 defensive contributions). And yet, maybe the most interesting bit about Tori's team this week is that she didn't have a single 0 or 1 pointer. I'm honestly not sure the last time I've seen that.

9 - Chris (-4) - League A - Last 2: 54 (W), 33 (L) - Kinda a rough one for Chris as his highest score came from 7 whole points from Benjamin White. Hume also kept a clean sheet for what it's worth. Hey and the best to ever do it Harry Maguire used that big brain of his to turn what would've been a handball into an Ipswich own goal so I'd say things are looking preeeetty good for Chris going forward. Anyway, surely Brobbey will get it going soon and Hinshelwood finds some magic injury spray somewhere.

8 - Luke (+1) - League A - Last 2: 53 (W), 45 (W) - Despite trying to trade his entire team away, Luke's last couple of weeks look eerily similar to Tori's, but with none of the same players. His score this week came from his triplet of defcon beasts: Ndiaye, Mykolenko, and Wirtz? Oh wait that's an assist and yellow my bad. His high scorer this week was Gross (of course) backed up by a couple of goals from Wissa and Cold Palmer. Plus, maybe if he didn't trust Spurs players so much he'd have another 10 points this week.

7 - Kim (-1) - League B - Last 2: 38 (L), 47 (L) - A tough matchup this week for Kim as Haaland's brace just wasn't enough to save her from triple double digit returns on the other side of the field. Anyway, 47 is still pretty solid as Haaland was joined by Foden's double assist, Thiaw picked up a clean sheet, and Xhaka somehow got 6 points from a clean sheet and 3 bonus, in a 1-0 game. Huh. Despite the couple of losses, Oli McBurnie cannot be stopped so I expect to see a long line of (W) from here on out.

6 - Charlotte (+1) - League C - Last 2: 50 (D), 50 (W) - The bastion of consistency herself with back to back 50 pointers, this time including 10 from Schade's goal, 11 from Mbeumo and Lewis Hall, and not much else going on. But hey, 50 points is 50 points, and Igor Jesus has just passed 1 xG and is still at 0 goals which means he is guaranteed a goal next week. That's what that means, right?

5 - Katie (+3) - League C - Last 2: 30 (L), 80 (W) - She had me doubting myself after seeing the GW1 30 pointer after I put her in this slot after the draft, but boy was I vindicated this week as she puts up what could certainly be a season-high 80 pointer (based on last season). And surprise surprise, 23 of those points came from Bruno Fernandes. Add in the Scott and Tarkowski 12 pointers, MGW's 13, and Isidor and Evanilson going full Power Rangers and hyphenating into one Gibbs-White and you've got quite the squad. Plus, 3 of her players didn't even start, and if she trusted Hull City a little bit more than Liverpool, it could've been a 90. And then we're getting into Matthew season 2 territory.

4 - Andrew (-3) - League B - Last 2: 53 (L), 28 (L) - What goes up comes back down as Tavernier/Dango/Aaronson/Lacroix/Canvot/Kerkez/Verbruggen tried their hardest and combined for an impressive 5 points. Which just so happens to be the same amount as Rogers and also Evanilson. Does that mean Evanilson is worth the same as all of those players? Probably. If only Joao Pedro had scored a hat trick of own goals instead of just 1 and he would've been worth 1 Evanilson as well. There's always next week.

3 - Zach (-1) - League A - Last 2: 47 (W), 45 (L) - Unfortunately for Zach he came up against the unstoppable force that is my team this week, but I'm sure he'll bounce back considering he's got the best goalscorer in the league and Haaland. Jokes aside still a solid score this week from a slew of defensive contributions from Rice/Stach/Hill and one of these days a Sesko substitution is going to pay dividends like it was doing last season.

2 - Aarushi (+1) - League C - Last 2: 55 (W), 51 (L) - One of the few (as in, 1 of 3) managers with 2 50+ weeks to their name, Aarushi got hit by the Tori truck this week and lost by a single heartbreaking point. Other than Gross's 13 pointer, this 51 pointer was built from a whole lot of 4s, a 6, and an 8, as Wissa and Raya held up their ends of the bargain with a goal and a clean sheet, respectively, and Ndiaye, Sangare, Muharemovic, and Maguire did their part by hitting the defcon threshold. Wait, Maguire got an assist and that's how he ended up with 4? Am I allowed to make the same joke twice?

1 - Gavin (+3) - League B - Last 2: 63 (W), 52 (W) - The preseason odds for League B promotion seemed to be just about right on the money as Gavin continues his hot start with 2/2 wins and 2/2 weeks above 50 as Cherki found out goals are worth more than assists, Tarkowski remembered he can head the ball goalwards (I'm assuming he scored a header, I didn't see the game), and Isak scored a goal! Hooray for Isak owners, we're so back! Anyway, Gavin does have the Ndiaye -> Manchester City situation to figure out and if Tzolis 45' 0 pointers become a consistent thing he may have to drop 1 of the 3 stashed players he has. Or not, he's got Rayan Cherki and Wesley Fofana. He's good.
