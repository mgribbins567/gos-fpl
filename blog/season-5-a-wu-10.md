10 - JohnM (NEW) - League C - Last 5: 60 (W), 35 (W), 32 (L), 56 (W), 45 (W) - Welcome to the list, John(the M version)! John is coming off 2 solid 45+ weeks, has a 60 pointer in the last 5, and has been kinda flying in League C as of late with only 1 loss in the last 6. Is 2 good weeks in a row enough to get on this list? Maybe? It is like 11pm on Thursday and I need to finish this up so for the #10 slot this week, it is. And, believe it or not, JohnM holds the (tied) second highest low score in League C, which is also pretty good. Plus, these last few slots _should_ change a lot week to week (probably?).

9 - Chris (-1) - League A - Last 5: 43 (W), 46 (W), 46 (W), 37 (W), 36 (L) - As Chris's PF starts slowly tapering off, it seems he _is_ capable of losing at middle-upper 30s scores. Which isn't too promising for his future on this list, so I'll be watching his gameweek 10 performance closely. I do think this position is recoverable, since there's definitely precedent for streaks in the 30s on this list, especially mid-30s. Double especially if they include wins, since getting lucky is also something of a skill it seems.

8 - Amber (-2) - League B - Last 5: 25 (L), 33 (W), 60 (W), 52 (W), 31 (L) - As termed by Amber, it would appear that she is in, as termed by me, a bust week. Unfortunate, because her team had the potential to really shoot up the rankings with another couple results. Instead, I have to figure out what to do with scores that look like this ^. Is her team really good? Is it bad? Surely the Arsenal reliance isn't the problem, considering this week included a triple Arsenal clean sheet. But also, does anyone really _want_ to go up against this team any given week? Not really.

7 - Coop (-3) - League A - Last 5: 36 (L), 52 (W), 43 (W), 44 (W), 30 (L) - Yikes, seems like maybe the Power Rankings curse hit a second manager on this list as Coop put up his own 2nd lowest score of the season this week. Could be a blip, could be that he didn't draft enough Tottenham players so the FPL gods smote him while he was sitting in the stands. Either way, despite underrating Coop's team for so long, I need to be a _little_ more flexible with these rankings so down he goes. For now.

6 - Jesse (NEW) - League A - Last 5: 28 (L), 28 (L), 54 (W), 72 (W), 51 (W) - Not really sure how or why Jesse's team went from scoring 28 to 50+ every week, but obviously he's doing something right these last 3 weeks. Maybe it's the picking up Luke's ill-advised waiver drops, maybe it's just Oli McBurnie playing out of his gourd, or maybe it's just good ol' fashioned FPL luck. Regardless, he's here now and apparently everyone new to the list just gets plopped in the middle so good luck!

5 - ChrisB (+2) - League B - Last 5: 68 (W), 35 (W), 32 (W), 46 (W), 51 (W) - That's 5 wins in a row for ChrisB as he claims the "longest winstreak in the leagues" trophy this week after other Chris's streak was broken. And with the 2nd highest score in League B as well (by 1 point), it was enough to keep him trending upward on this list. Which he should be happy to see compared to his competitor at the top of League B who has taken up residency in the "fell off" section.

4 - Zach (+1) - League A - Last 5: 40 (W), 44 (L), 56 (W), 28 (L), 40 (W) - Not the most convincing score but good enough to keep Zach in the top half as he beat the average by exactly 1 point. But also, despite the score last week, he's been one of the most consistent managers in recent weeks, with 5 out of the last 6 weeks at 40+. Consistent enough to top the xP table outright, in fact. I guess I'm in 3rd on that table though so you can take that fact how you want. _I_ think it's probably the most important table there is.

3 - Andrew (-2) - League B - Last 5: 41 (W), 50 (W), 59 (W), 43 (L), 45 (L) - Remember how I mentioned that luck is a skill? Well, Andrew might need to get to practicing. That's now a loss at 43, 45, and 53 points for Andrew which is absolutely brutal. In League B, Kim is the only other manager with more than 1 loss when scoring 40+, with 2 such instances. (MatthewR and Zach both have 2 in League A, while Maddie has a draw and a loss at 40+ in League C, if that counts) Either way, Andrew's only spent 2 weeks out of 9 this season below 30, so I think he'll be fine, but also back to back losses while scoring in the 40s is wild.

2 - Dylan (+1) - League A - 38 (L), 51 (W), 41 (W), 54 (L), 53 (W) - And onto the other manager who has seen some bad luck in the past couple of weeks, Dylan! Although, unlike Andrew, Dylan won this week and has notched 3 50+ scores in the last 5 weeks and doesn't appear to be slowing down. Which is a pretty marked improvement on the up and down first 5 weeks of the season in which whenever he'd score in the 30s it'd come with a loss. Now the heartbreak can be even more brutal! Anyway, he's entered the echelon of impressive runs that without a doubt deserve to be near the top of the list, so it's only fighting for the top spot from here.

1 - Tori (+1) - League C - 36 (L), 66 (W), 46 (W), 61 (W), 60 (W) - I mean, what a run Tori has been on. The highest score in League C in 3 of the last 4 weeks, with all 3 of those being in the 60s. We haven't seen a run like that since probably me in Season 2? I don't even think Matthew2 has a comparable streak. (just checked, yeah probably the 78, 55, 78, 84, 95 run in S2 would beat this one slightly) Anyway, that certainly doesn't take away from Tori's achievement here, especially when considering that _none_ of her players had double digits this week. And also she had a player with 0 minutes _on the field_. Just crazy consistency across the board over there and absolutely deserving of the top spot right now.
