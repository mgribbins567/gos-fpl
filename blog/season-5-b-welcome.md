Hello everyone and welcome to Game of Stones Season 5! I'm incredibly hyped for this season as we have some _big_ changes that I think are going to make this season more fun, competitive, and just generally interesting than we've had before!

# The Website (gosfpl.com)

But it won't be without some hiccups, because we're **fully transitioning** to <https://www.gosfpl.com/> for all things FPL. Yep, that means you won't need to use that pesky Premier League app again. However, it does mean that there _will_ be bugs. I am human and have a job (that isn't in web dev, so please bear with my UI/UX shortcomings) so I can only dedicate so much time to polishing, fixing, and adding the nice-to-have features that I want to add.

And if you run into anything that looks weird, doesn't function how you expect, or needs a little bit of attention, tell me! I am **always** looking for feedback good and bad and have a looooong list of things I'd like to add to the website, so what's one more on top. But also, don't tell me until _after_ the season starts, since I have a very dedicated team of beta testers clicking lots of buttons and shooting gameweek numbers off screen until then.

Anyway, your mission is to go to <https://www.gosfpl.com/>, click the "Sign in" button, and sign up for an account! Once signed in, you should see something that says "Welcome - ping me to link your account" (or something to that extent). I'll be going in every couple days or so and manually linking the account you create to your "Manager" (remember, limited time for automation), which will cause that line to update to something like "Hello Matthew." Once that's happened, you're all set up website-wise. And if you run into any issues, let me know so I can make sure other people don't.

# Mid-Season Tournaments

A quick note since a few of y'all are new: I run mid-season tournaments throughout the year (sort of mimicking the Champions League, FA Cup, etc.). It'll be fairly obvious these are going on if you keep up with the weekly updates, but basically some weeks your team will play 2 matches, one in the league and one in the cup. That means your score will be used against two different opponents, which means sometimes you can lose in the league and win in the cup. Makes for a nice reward if you get really unlucky in the league yet still are able to defeat your cup opponent.

# Promotion / Relegation

And since there are some new people here, League B has both promotion places and relegation spots! The top 2 finishers will be **automatically** promoted to League A for Season 6, while the 3rd place manager will go into a playoff against the 10th place manager in League A in Gameweek 37 and 38 (this is done "retroactively," but we'll keep track of the possible matchups in those weeks).

League B also has relegation spots to League C, which for this season will act the same way. 11th and 12th get automatically relegated, while 10th goes into a playoff against League C 3rd place.
