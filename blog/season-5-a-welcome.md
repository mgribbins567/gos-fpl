Hello everyone and welcome to Game of Stones Season 5! I'm incredibly hyped for this season as we have some _big_ changes that I think are going to make this season more fun, competitive, and just generally interesting than we've had before!

# The Website (gosfpl.com)

But it won't be without some hiccups, because we're **fully transitioning** to <https://www.gosfpl.com/> for all things FPL. Yep, that means you won't need to use that pesky Premier League app again. However, it does mean that there _will_ be bugs. I am human and have a job (that isn't in web dev, so please bear with my UI/UX shortcomings) so I can only dedicate so much time to polishing, fixing, and adding the nice-to-have features that I want to add.

And if you run into anything that looks weird, doesn't function how you expect, or needs a little bit of attention, tell me! I am **always** looking for feedback good and bad and have a looooong list of things I'd like to add to the website, so what's one more on top. But also, don't tell me until _after_ the season starts, since I have a very dedicated team of beta testers clicking lots of buttons and shooting gameweek numbers off screen until then.

Anyway, your mission is to go to <https://www.gosfpl.com/>, click the "Sign in" button, and sign up for an account! Once signed in, you should see something that says "Welcome - ping me to link your account" (or something to that extent). I'll be going in every couple days or so and manually linking the account you create to your "Manager" (remember, limited time for polish), which will cause that line to update to something like "Hello Matthew." Once that's happened, you're all set up website-wise. And if you run into any issues, let me know so I can make sure other people don't.

# Draft Day

The other thing I need from you is to fill out the when2meet for the draft! The draft will take place August 15th or 16th depending on the league. League A appears to be at 11:15/11:45am Pacific Time for now: <https://www.when2meet.com/?37257572-826KE/>

And if you can't make the draft, that's okay! Send me an ordered draft list so that I can draft on your behalf. Since we're doing the draft offline, I'll send out a spreadsheet for draft day a week or so in advance which you can make a copy of to edit and share with me, or just do it in a Google doc/txt file/text message/by hand and send it over. Feel free to add if statements (if I have 2 DEFs stop drafting DEFs until round 10), or whatever else you can think of.

And speaking of draft day, the draft will probably be a chain of Google Meets (since the room only stays open for ~1 hour iirc) unless I can figure out a better solution. I'll send the link out to everyone morning of, _please_ be prompt!

I think that's about it (and for reference, this will go similarly to how I outlined it [last year](https://www.gosfpl.com/blog/season-4-draft-day)), but don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions!
