**What if I run out of time during my turn, miss the draft, or have to leave early?**

There are two options in these cases:

1. Prepare a watchlist (which should be sent to me by draft time), in which case I will announce the next ranked available player on your behalf.
2. The next highest draft rank player will be selected for you (meeting position requirements, etc.)

FYI- draft rank is based on Price, then Total Points from last season

**What happens after the draft?**

The Premier League officially starts on Friday, August 21st. After the draft, I'll add your drafted players to your team on <https://www.gosfpl.com/>, where you will be able to put in waivers, make trades, and choose your starting lineup for Gameweek 1!
